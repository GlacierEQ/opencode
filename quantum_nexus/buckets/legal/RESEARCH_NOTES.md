## CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

### 14th Amendment - Due Process
- **Cite:** U.S. Const. amend. XIV, § 1
- **Summary:** No state shall deprive any person of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law
- **Application:** Family court custody proceedings must provide:
  - Adequate notice
  - Opportunity to be heard
  - Impartial tribunal
  - Right to present evidence
  - Right to counsel

### 5th Amendment - Due Process
- **Cite:** U.S. Const. amend. V
- **Summary:** No person shall be deprived of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law
- **Application:** Federal protections apply to state actions via 14th Amendment

### 6th Amendment - Right to Counsel
- **Cite:** U.S. Const. amend. VI
- **Summary:** In all criminal prosecutions, the accused shall have the assistance of counsel
- **Application:** May extend to dependency proceedings where liberty interest is at stake

### 1st Amendment - Freedom of Speech
- **Cite:** U.S. Const. amend. I
- **Summary:** Congress shall make no law abridging the freedom of speech
- **Application:** Protects parent's right to speak about custody proceedings

## HAWAII STATUTES

### HRS 571 - Family Court
- **Cite:** Haw. Rev. Stat. § 571
- **Summary:** Establishes Family Court jurisdiction
- **Key Sections:**
  - § 571-2: Jurisdiction
  - § 571-46: Custody
  - § 571-47: Visitation
  - § 571-52: Support

### HRS 571-46 - Custody
- **Cite:** Haw. Rev. Stat. § 571-46
- **Summary:** Best interest of the child standard
- **Factors:**
  - Child's preference (if of sufficient age)
  - Parent's ability to provide
  - Stability of home environment
  - Relationship with child

### HRS 571-47 - Visitation
- **Cite:** Haw. Rev. Stat. § 571-47
- **Summary:** Reasonable visitation rights
- **Application:** Must be in best interest of child

### HRS 571-52 - Support
- **Cite:** Haw. Rev. Stat. § 571-52
- **Summary:** Child support obligations
- **Application:** Based on income shares model

## WRIT OPTIONS

### Habeas Corpus
- **Purpose:** Challenge unlawful detention
- **Requirements:**
  - Person detained
  - Detention unlawful
  - No adequate remedy at law
- **Filing:** Petition for Writ of Habeas Corpus
- **Court:** Hawaii Family Court or Circuit Court

### Mandamus
- **Purpose:** Compel official duty
- **Requirements:**
  - Official has duty to act
  - Duty is ministerial
  - Refusal to act
  - No other adequate remedy
- **Filing:** Petition for Writ of Mandamus
- **Court:** Hawaii Circuit Court

### Prohibition
- **Purpose:** Prevent court overreach
- **Requirements:**
  - Court exceeds jurisdiction
  - No appeal adequate remedy
  - Irreparable harm
- **Filing:** Petition for Writ of Prohibition
- **Court:** Hawaii Supreme Court or Intermediate Appellate Court

### Certiorari
- **Purpose:** Review lower court decision
- **Requirements:**
  - Final judgment
  - Error of law
  - No adequate remedy
- **Filing:** Petition for Writ of Certiorari
- **Court:** Hawaii Supreme Court
